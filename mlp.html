Introduction

Under Sandia National Laboratories, I researched machine learning potentials (MLPs) to investigate whether magnetism impacts the accuracy of the model. The work was split between two researchers: I handled the non-magnetic portion while a teammate handled the magnetic portion.

Methods

I developed and evaluated the non-magnetic models, establishing the baseline against which the magnetic results could be compared.

Results

The comparison laid the groundwork for continued research toward a definitive answer on whether magnetism affects MLP accuracy.

Discussion

I presented the research findings, translating a technical comparison into a clear narrative for a scientific audience. The project established a foundation that future work can build on to reach a final conclusion.

My Contributions

  • Owned the full non-magnetic portion of the study
  • Established the non-magnetic baseline for the magnetism comparison
  • Presented the research findings
  • Coordinated scope and methods with the teammate handling the magnetic portion
