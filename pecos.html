Introduction

As lead quality engineer, I directed the validation of the PECOS system at a Hill's Pet Nutrition processing plant. The goal was to verify the system performed reliably under current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) standards before release to production.

Methods

I used SAP and Kneat to determine the appropriate sampling plans for the validation protocol, defining how many samples were required and at which points in the process they would be pulled. Sampling was executed on the plant floor in coordination with production and quality teams.

Results

The validation data were compiled into a cGMP-compliant report documenting that the system met its acceptance criteria.

Discussion

This project reinforced how validation work depends on collaboration across quality, production, and documentation teams — and how structured tools like SAP and Kneat keep a multi-department effort auditable from start to finish.

My Contributions

  • Served as lead quality engineer for the full validation effort
  • Determined sampling plans using SAP and Kneat
  • Generated the final cGMP validation report
  • Coordinated with cross-functional plant teams to execute sampling
